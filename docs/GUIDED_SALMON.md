# Guided oven salmon

Selecting salmon and oven roasting opens five choices: thawing state, portion size,
thickness, covered/open roasting and seasoning. The final answer starts the plan
without another start or add-action request. Frozen fish receives a refrigerator
thawing step. Both `סלמון` and `סלומון` are recognized.

Instructions specify oven mode, shelf, tray preparation, per-portion seasoning,
skin orientation, covering, measurement and serving. A reported measurement of at
least 63°C is required to advance from the cooking step to serving; a measurement
from before that step is cleared. Completing the session is not safety certification.

The technique reference is [Good Food baked salmon](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/baked-salmon).
Seasoning variants are authored adaptations, not verbatim versions of that recipe.
Handling and the temperature check follow [FDA seafood guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-fresh-and-frozen-seafood-safely).
Both links appear next to the instructions. Times remain estimates, not readiness checks.

The three-column workbench stays visible. Existing manual plans are preserved.
